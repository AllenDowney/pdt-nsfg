None. Two moved into the standard:
- `nsfg.variable_type` became `variable_type` (PublicDataToolkit Task 25), mapped by the rule `nsfg_variable_type`.
- `nsfg.item`, the questionnaire item, became `questions[].number` (PublicDataToolkit Task 43): from the 2017-2019 codebook's item reference, or the 2022-2023 questionnaire's.
